# Research

The two source documents behind everything in this repo, as authored.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `taxfix-dossier.html` | The company dossier — mission, leadership, financials, the three-front squeeze, documented product gaps, the Builder model, and the seven candidate surfaces. Open in a browser. |
| `validation-playbook.html` | Print source for the playbook below. |
| `Taxfix-Validation-Playbook.pdf` | 12 pages, A4. One page per idea: the pain with evidence, oracle, test set, metric, declared pass bar, failure mode, and a jury defence with prepared objections. |

Condensed versions live in `docs/DOSSIER.md` and `docs/PLAYBOOK.md` — those are
what the `brownie` skill reads at speed.

All research is from public sources, gathered 16 Sep 2026. Financial figures come
from German trade press reporting on filed accounts. Anything marked as inference
in the documents is inference, not reported fact.
